Set up this phone

This phone will pass your shop's M-Pesa payment messages on to NexaPOS, so your tills can check a customer's M-Pesa code against the real message.

Only payments you receive are kept. Your balance, and anything you send or withdraw, is dropped unread.

This page has no key. On your till open Settings > M-Pesa Till Phone > Set up a phone, then scan the code it shows with this phone's camera.

1. Your address and key

You will paste these two into the shortcut below. Keep the key to yourself: it is what lets this phone report payments to your shop.

2. On an iPhone: make the shortcut

The pictures are from an iPhone with iOS 27. The red numbers show where to tap.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to make a new shortcut.
  2. Tap Edit, then Automation, and choose Message. A block "When I receive a message" appears.
  3. In that block, set Message contains to Ksh (picture A, 1 - the picture still shows the older word Confirmed). Every M-Pesa, M-PESA Jumbe and bank payment message has Ksh in it; any other message the phone passes on is dropped by the shop and nothing of it is kept.
  4. Tap Search at the bottom (picture A, 2), type Get Contents of URL and tap it to add it.
  5. Tap its URL and paste the address from step 1 (use Copy address first).
  1. Tap the small arrow at the end of the address (picture B, 1). Method, Headers and Request Body appear.
  1. Tap GET next to Method and choose POST (picture C, 3). Request Body must say JSON (4).
  2. Tap Add new field and choose Text. In its left box type key - tap ⇧ first so the k stays small. In its right box paste the key from step 1 (use Copy key first) (5).
  3. Tap Add new field again and choose Text. In its left box type text (small t). Tap its right box but do not type: tap Message above the keyboard, so a blue Message bubble appears (6).
  4. Still under Request Body, tap Add new field a third time and choose Text. In its left box type sender (small s). Tap its right box, tap Message above the keyboard so a blue bubble appears, then tap that blue bubble and choose Sender: the bubble in this row must say Sender (the text row keeps Message). Now the shop knows who each SMS came from, and refuses a message that a person typed and sent from their own number. Do not use Add Filter in the "When I receive a message" block for this: a Sender filter there switches the shortcut off.
  5. Check that Automation is on (1) and Confirm Before Run is off (2), so the shortcut runs by itself.
  6. Tap the back arrow at the top to save.
  7. Banks that write KES: Equity Bank writes KES, not Ksh, so its messages need a second copy of the shortcut. In the Shortcuts list, press and hold this shortcut and tap Duplicate. Open the copy, change Message contains from Ksh to Confirmed, and check that its Automation is on and Confirm Before Run is off. An M-Pesa message has both words, so it is passed on twice: the shop keeps it once.
The three names must be exactly key, text and sender: the key in the first row, the blue Message bubble in the second and the blue Sender bubble in the third. (Picture C was taken before the third row was added.)

Already set up a phone before? Open its shortcut, change Message contains from Confirmed to Ksh (so bank messages such as Co-op Bank's are passed on too), and add the sender row (step 10) if it is not there yet, and make the second copy for Equity Bank (step 13). On the till, Settings > M-Pesa Till Phone shows Sender check on for that phone after its next M-Pesa message.

On an older iPhone (iOS 17 to 26): open Shortcuts, tap Automation, then +, choose Message, set Message contains to Ksh and choose Run Immediately. Then add the same Get Contents of URL step (steps 4 to 10).

If a step looks different on your phone, send a screenshot to NexaPOS support.

3. Try it

Lock this phone and check your M-Pesa balance, so that a message arrives. Then on the till open Settings > M-Pesa Till Phone and tap Refresh: this phone shows Last message with the time (picture D). A balance is not a payment, so it is not listed under Payments received.

When a customer pays your till or paybill, the payment shows on the till under Payments received a few seconds after the message reaches this phone.

Keep this phone switched on and connected to the internet during shop hours.

On an Android phone

The NexaPOS app passes the messages on by itself - no shortcut to make. It needs NexaPOS 1.0.95 or later.

  1. Install NexaPOS on this phone: open nexapos.cc and download it for Android.
  2. Tap Copy key above.
  3. Open NexaPOS. On a phone that only gets the M-Pesa messages, tap This phone only passes on M-Pesa messages on its first screen. (On a phone that is already a till of your shop: Settings > M-Pesa Till Phone > M-Pesa messages from this phone.)
  4. Paste the key and tap Turn on. When Android asks, allow NexaPOS to receive text messages.
  5. If Android says Restricted setting (or does not ask at all): tap Open App info in NexaPOS, then the three dots at the top right > Allow restricted settings, then Permissions > SMS > Allow. Go back and tap Turn on again.

Only messages from MPESA and banks that mention Ksh, KES or Confirmed leave the phone - never a message from a person's number. It works with NexaPOS closed; without internet the messages wait and go later.

You can go back and forth between Shortcuts and this page: this phone remembers the key for 2 hours, then forgets it. Close this page when you have finished.
