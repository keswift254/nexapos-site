Set up this phone

This phone will pass your shop's M-Pesa payment messages on to NexaPOS, so your tills can check a customer's M-Pesa code against the real message.

Only payments you receive are kept. Your balance, and anything you send or withdraw, is dropped unread.

This page has no key. On your till open Settings > M-Pesa Till Phone > Set up a phone, then scan the code it shows with this phone's camera.

1. Your address and key

You will paste these two into the shortcut below. Keep the key to yourself: it is what lets this phone report payments to your shop.

KEY

2. On an iPhone: make the shortcut

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to make a new shortcut.
  2. Tap Edit, then Automation, and choose Message.
  3. In the block that appears, set Message contains to Confirmed
  4. In Search at the bottom, type Get Contents of URL and add it.
  5. Tap its URL and paste the address from step 1.
  6. Open that block's extra settings (the small arrow). Set Method to POST and Request Body to JSON.
  7. Add a Text field. Name it key and paste the key from step 1 as its value.
  8. Add another Text field. Name it text. For its value choose the message that was received (shown as Shortcut Input or Message).
  9. Tap the ⓘ button, then Privacy, and turn on Allow Running When Locked.
  10. Tap Done.

On an older iPhone (iOS 17 to 26): open Shortcuts, tap Automation, then +, choose Message, set Message contains to Confirmed and choose Run Immediately. Then add the same Get Contents of URL step.

If a step looks different on your phone, send a screenshot to NexaPOS support.

3. Try it

Check your M-Pesa balance, so that a message arrives. Within a minute, the till shows this phone with Last message and the time, under Settings > M-Pesa Till Phone.

When a customer pays your till or paybill, the payment shows on the till under Payments received a few seconds after the message reaches this phone.

Keep this phone switched on and connected to the internet during shop hours.
